Partner selection

Selected partnersHairdresser Hub (6) · Brewed Awakening (1) · Sole Trader Money (7)
Rejected partnerBest Card Deals UK (3)

I used the benchmark of £50 per new activated customer to analyse each partner. I also used my own experience to decide which partner is best. For example, paid traffic is usually lower quality than organic traffic, podcast listeners are easier to convert, and podcast hosts carry more authority with their audience.

Note on the benchmark: the brief says €50, but the UK market and all nine applicants' terms are in £. I treated this as a typo and used £50 throughout. If euros were intended (about £43), my ranking of the partners would not change.

Full-year plan

Hairdresser Hub: a gated 2027 podcast plan for UK salon owners. Each quarter must earn the next.

Q1
Jan to Mar

March: card reader launch

Prove that the audience converts before committing more spend.

Products

Card readers (Payments & POS)

Content and format

One host-read podcast episode with a real salon payment story and a tracked link in the show notes

Metrics to track

Listens at day 7 and 30, UK share, clicks, eligible activations, cost per activation

Q2
Apr to Jun

May: prepare for summer

Only if Q1 passes. Help salons get ready for the busy season.

Products

Card readers, Payment Links (deposits, remote payments)

Content and format

Host-read episode plus a short written guide on the partner website

Metrics to track

Reach, UK audience, click-to-activation rate, valid cost, listener feedback

Q3
Jul to Sep

September: back to business

Reach owners who are catching up on cash flow and admin.

Products

Business account and invoicing, only where they fit

Content and format

Episode on cash flow and getting paid, with a checklist download

Metrics to track

Activations, product mix, conversion lag, cost, accuracy of claims

Q4
Oct to Dec

November: Black Friday

Use the busiest trading period to take more payments.

Products

Card reader and approved tools. Discounts only if approved

Content and format

Seasonal episode, partner email and social mention

Metrics to track

Day 30 reach, activations, cost, reversals, incremental value

Reporting

From partner: listens, geography, audience profile
From Impact: clicks, signups, eligible activations, reversals, total cost
Rhythm: day 7 read, day 30 review, quarterly decision

Scale

UK audience verified, tracking works, and activation cost and quality meet the target.

Optimise

Reach good, clicks low: improve the host read and CTA. Clicks good, activations low: check page, offer and tracking.

Pause

Audience cannot be verified, reporting missing, tracking broken, claims wrong or cost above limit.

Part 4 of 4

Biggest risk

The plan must deliver incremental UK customers, not just reach.

The risk

Paying for reach, not customers

Listeners, visits or engagement that never become eligible, activated UK small business customers.

01
Early warning signs
  • Low UK audience share
  • Reach without tracked clicks
  • Clicks that do not become activations
  • Traffic that looks too good to be true
  • Partner numbers that do not match Impact
02
First action
  • Before launch: verify audience, agree activation and attribution, test every Impact link
  • After launch: reconcile partner reports with Impact; check tracking, page and offer before blaming conversion
  • Hold new paid commitments until clear
03
Final call
  • Affiliate Manager / Partnerships Lead decides: continue, renegotiate or pause
  • Affiliate Ops validates tracking
  • Finance advises on acquisition cost
  • The intern gathers evidence and flags, but does not approve spend

Scale

when qualified, incremental customers arrive at an acceptable cost

Optimise

when the bottleneck is fixable (page, offer, CTA)

Pause

when audience, tracking, compliance or economics cannot be validated
